Tap a cell for the matchup details. Numbers are the row deck's win rate.

Win rate is the 3-point rate (win 3, tie 1, loss 0), as Trainer Hill reports it. Ties pull it slightly below 50% even when wins equal losses, so even / uneven is judged by the edge: win rate minus that matchup's break-even, which works out to (wins − losses) ÷ (2 × games). ± is a rough 95% margin of error. Few games means a noisy number, so raise Min games when in doubt. Mirror matches are left out. Share is the % of players on the archetype in the selected events.
